2021Personal project

Neural networks learning to play Snake and the Chrome Dino game

Neuroevolution training of neural networks to play Snake and the Google Chrome dinosaur game, both recreated in Python with Pygame. In each generation, a whole population plays at the same time (over 300 snakes, dozens of dinosaurs) and the best individuals pass their genes on to the next generation. The display tracks the generation number and how many individuals are still alive in real time.

  • Python
  • Pygame
  • Neural networks
